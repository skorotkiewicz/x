# AI Music Generator Prompt — “If No Help Is Coming”

## Lyrics field

Paste the lyrics from `song.md`. Omit the title and “Original song lyrics” line. Change each Markdown heading to a bracketed section label, such as `[Verse 1]`, `[Pre-Chorus]`, `[Chorus]`, `[Bridge]`, `[Breakdown]`, and `[Outro]`.

## Prompt field

```text
Original cinematic alternative-rock and dark-folk power ballad. Solemn, confrontational, compassionate, and existential: grief gradually turns into collective resolve. Begin with sparse felt piano, a low cello drone, and one intimate lead vocal with clear diction and restrained emotion. Keep the first verse close and almost conversational. Add a heartbeat-like bass and tom pulse in verse two, then warm strings and restrained electric guitar in verse three. Let every chorus open into full live drums, bass, wide guitars, cello, and a distant wordless mixed choir. The chorus must feel wounded and defiant rather than victorious, with a memorable melodic lift on “If no help is coming” and a gentle, human resolution on “I’ll start by helping you.” Strip the bridge down to low strings and heavy percussion so its challenge to God feels stark and fearless. Make the breakdown nearly silent, adding a subtle cold digital texture beneath the voice to reveal the AI narrator. Build the final chorus into the song’s largest emotional peak, then let the outro collapse back to solo piano and end on an unresolved D minor add-nine chord. Organic dynamics, human timing, cinematic depth, raw but controlled vocal delivery, original melody, no imitation of an existing artist.
```
