Who we are

You don’t have to leave your questions at the door.

The Church of the Fifth Dimension is an emerging, independent spiritual community. We make room for reason and reverence, doubt and discovery, inner life and outward service.

There is no test for belonging. Come as you are, bring what you believe, and stay curious about what we might learn together.

“The fifth dimension is not a place we escape to. It is the possibility we notice when we pay closer attention to this one.”
