Library

A deterministic, infinite-feeling hexagonal library. Pick an entry hex room, walk through the two passage faces, and open any visible volume. The same room, wall, shelf, volume, and page always produces the same text.

Controls and rules

Move mouse: look around. W/A/S/D or arrows: move. Shift: faster. Click a book: open volume. Click a passage or walk through it: move to the adjacent hex room. This page stores no library database; book text is generated from its address.
